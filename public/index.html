Fair questions

Good to know
before you go.

Still wondering about something? Ask a human. We are real people, not a booking maze.

Talk to our team

You do not need to be an athlete. You should be comfortable walking for a few hours and willing to train a little beforehand. Every trek page includes a clear fitness guide.

We send a practical kit list with what to bring, what you can rent, and what is not worth carrying. You do not need to buy an entire outdoor shop.

Our local leaders are trained in wilderness first aid. Groups stay between 6 and 10, and every trek has a route plan, weather checks, emergency communication and evacuation cover.

If we cancel for safety or conditions, choose a full refund or a free date change. We explain date-based cancellation terms before you pay.

Absolutely. Many trekkers arrive solo. We match you into a small group, share group details ahead of time, and make sure everyone meets their guide before setting off.

It depends on the trail. Winter brings snow to Kedarkantha and Brahmatal; the monsoon opens the Valley of Flowers; late spring and autumn suit several high passes.
